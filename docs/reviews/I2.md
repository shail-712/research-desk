## Findings
1. **MUST — `docs/RESUME-FACTS.md:267-269` (§5 bullet 2): the "multi-agent" claim carries a number that the
   multi-agent answers don't meet.**
   - The bullet says "a Gemini supervisor orchestrates parallel market, news and SEC-filings agents … 90% of
     answers returned within 21 seconds".
   - On that eval set, only **4 of 20** Deep answers used more than one specialist (c5, w1, w2, w4). 10 skipped
     the supervisor (fast path). 6 of the 10 "full pipeline" answers also ran a single specialist (c1, c2, c3, c4,
     c6, w3).
   - The 4 multi-specialist answers took 12.8, 13.6, 21.8 and 25.3 s, so 2 of 4 were over 21 s. The full
     pipeline's p90 is 21.8 s.
   - The supporting facts (`:278-281`) call the full-pipeline 10 "the multi-agent path", which is overstated in
     the same way. So does `§1` `:17` ("TRUE (Deep mode only)").
   - Fix: put the latency on the mode, not the architecture. For example: "…with a single-agent fast path for
     simple questions; Deep-mode answers returned within 21 s for 90% of a 20-question eval set". In the
     supporting facts, add "4 of 20 used 2+ specialists in parallel; the brief's 3-company query ran all three in
     15.8 s". Add the same caveat to `:17`.
2. **MUST — `docs/RESUME-FACTS.md:262-263` (§5 bullet 1): "deployed answers cited sources on 91% of numeric
   lines".**
   - 91% is Quick only. Deep is 82%, and all answers together are 253/291 = 87%.

     comparisons; numbers and v1 belong in the supporting facts.
   - Fix: "Quick-mode answers cited sources on 91% of numeric lines" (or "87% across both modes"). Say "eval set".
     Move "v1: 44%" into the "How does it work?" line.
